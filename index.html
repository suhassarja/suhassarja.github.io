~/projects/nlite · May 2026
Flagship build

Nlite — Cognitive Assessment Platform

Architected end-to-end: a React Native app, two Vue 3 dashboards, and a Node.js/Hapi.js microservices backend with dual PostgreSQL/MongoDB. Built the AI layer — Gemini + Sarvam pipelines for report generation, OCR essay grading, and speech scoring — with a deterministic safety validator on every AI-written report.

  • Handwriting/essay grading: AWS Textract OCR + Gemini rubric-based scoring for structure, grammar, coherence.
  • Speech scoring: Sarvam AI speech-to-text + a hybrid LCS-diff/Gemini semantic reconciliation to fairly score paraphrased answers.
  • 20+ real-time cognitive games on a dedicated scoring microservice, with an AES-256-encrypted, mathjs-based formula interpreter and async Bull/Redis processing.
  • Wati (WhatsApp Business API) integration to deliver CBCL assessments directly to parents.
  • Docker/GitHub Actions CI/CD with path-filtered deploys to AWS EC2, plus k6 load/stress/soak test suites.
